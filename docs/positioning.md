# Positioning

## Category

Doubt is a **source-grounded decision artifact** for AI agents.

It is smaller than a research platform, stricter than a diagram generator, and
more portable than a collaborative argument-map application. The product wedge
is an Agent Skill plus a deterministic JSON validator and self-contained HTML
renderer.

## One-line pitch

See what supports a conclusion, what contradicts it, and what is still missing.

## The enemy

Polished research prose that collapses disagreement and makes citations hard to
audit.

## Audience

1. Developers and technical leaders comparing consequential approaches.
2. Researchers and analysts reviewing contested claims.
3. Maintainers who want AI outputs to carry inspectable evidence.
4. Teams that need a provider-independent decision record in source control.

## Differentiation

| Alternative | What it does | Why Doubt is different |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research chatbot | Synthesizes a prose answer | Keeps contradiction, qualification, and unknowns visible |
| Knowledge graph | Connects related concepts | Requires a reasoning relation and note for every edge |
| Argument-map platform | Stores collaborative debate | Emits canonical JSON and portable HTML without an account |
| Diagram generator | Visualizes structure | Refuses unsourced or unused evidence |
| Fact checker | Assigns a verdict to claims | Maps how evidence changes a decision without claiming a truth oracle |

## Brand rules

- Voice: direct, calm, evidence-first.
- Avoid: “truth machine,” fake scientific precision, adversarial theater.
- Primary line: “See what supports a conclusion.”
- Secondary line: “If an answer matters, make its evidence navigable.”
- Visuals: near-black, acid green, coral contradiction, yellow unknowns, source
  regions beside the graph.

## Honest boundary

A valid map proves structural traceability, not truth. Sources may be wrong,
stale, copied, or misinterpreted. The product keeps those failure surfaces
visible; it does not pretend to eliminate them.
